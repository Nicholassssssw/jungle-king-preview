# Original tiger test placeholder

`tiger-test.glb` is generated by `scripts/build-test-assets.mjs`. It contains original geometric shapes and three animation channels: breathing, head nod and tail sway. Units are metres; the tiger is approximately 13 cm long. This scale is a test value, not a measured product dimension.

This model is a stylised test asset, not the game artwork, a product replica, or a biologically accurate depiction. It is offered under CC0 1.0 Universal: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . No external model, texture or bitmap is used.

The viewer can generate an iOS Quick Look USDZ from the GLB when no `ios-src` is supplied. The generated AR representation may be static. Android Scene Viewer animation and iOS Quick Look animation must be checked separately on devices; on-page GLB animation does not establish AR animation support.
