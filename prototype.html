02. Profiles & 3D assets

Animal stories come from species.json. Each piece ID connects a model label to a profile and optional GLB and USDZ files.

"pieceId": "tiger_piece",
"labels": ["tiger_piece", "tiger"],
"assets": {
  "glb": "/models/tiger.glb",
  "usdz": "/models/tiger.usdz"
}

The tiger card includes an original animated test model. Other animals use the artwork preview until their models are ready. Keep the source links and assessment scopes with every profile.

3D AR places a model in your space on supported devices. Attaching it to a moving chess piece needs additional tracking.
